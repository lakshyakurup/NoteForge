# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in NoteForge a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Standards

**Positive examples:** empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback.

**Unacceptable:** trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or ban contributors for violations.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
